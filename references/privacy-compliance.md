# Reference: Privacy + Compliance (joint Data/Security/Legal-promises)

Data classification drives handling: public, internal, personal, sensitive-personal, secret — each with collection purpose, retention lifetime, representations allowed, deletion duty. Consent/purpose promises treated as requirements (keeper): processing beyond purpose = REQUIREMENT/DEFECT, not growth hack. Subject rights (access/export/delete) as flows with identity proof scope (auth joint; export is egress sink in secret-flow sense).

Logging/analytics: minimization, pseudonymization with key authority, third-party processor inventory (subprocessors as supply chain). Cross-border/residency where repo declares. Audit logs for consequential access (who/what/when/generation — value-free where content sensitive). Compliance mappings (SOC2/GDPR/HIPAA etc.) only where repo declares regime — lanes never invent legal conclusions; they evidence controls and route legal questions to owners (QUESTION, legal owner). Findings cite control + evidence gap, never "non-compliant" legal verdicts.
